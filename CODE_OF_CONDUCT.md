# Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge

We are committed to providing a friendly, safe, and welcoming environment for all contributors, level of experience, identity, or background.

## Our Standards

Examples of behavior that contribute to a positive environment:

- Being kind and courteous to others.
- Giving and gracefully accepting constructive feedback.
- Focusing on what is best for the community.

## Enforcement

Instances of unacceptable behavior may be reported to the maintainers. All reports will be reviewed and handled with discretion.
